# Issue 03: Community Trail Publishing Moderation & Flywheel

Status: needs-info

## Context
There is an operational trade-off between instant traveler gratification after publishing a completed trail and preventing spam or low-quality itineraries from cluttering the curated feed.

## Options
1. **Instant Publish with Community Badge**: The trail is immediately visible under "Experienced by Community", with an option for Admins to later elevate to "Staff Verified" or unpublish.
2. **Mandatory Admin Approval First**: The trail is held in `pending_confirmation` until an admin reviews and approves it.
