# Issue 02: In-Transit Delays & Missed Check-Ins (Chai Stall Problem)

Status: needs-info

## Context
When a traveler stops at an untracked tea stall or shop for 30–45 minutes between scheduled stops, the planned timeline drifts out of sync and upcoming stops (such as temples closing at 1:00 PM) may be missed.

## Options
1. **Proactive Replan Prompt**: Detect transit delay (ETA + 25 mins without check-in) and offer a 1-tap timeline rebalance or push.
2. **Passive Delay**: Do not interrupt the traveler; leave timeline unadjusted until manual check-in or checkout.
