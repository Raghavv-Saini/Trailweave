---

## 🎭 Stage Setup & Choreography Rules

1. **The Stance:**
   - **Speaker 1 (Center-Left):** Owns the narrative, emotional hook, and big-picture vision. Stands tall, makes direct eye contact with Aayush, Vishal, and Bhavishya.
   - **Speaker 2 (Center-Right, next to screen/laptop):** The pilot. Controls the phone/trackpad with deliberate, steady movements. Never blocks the screen.
   - **Speaker 3 (Flanking Center):** The architect. Speaks with quiet confidence and technical authority.
2. **The "Apple Keynote" Hand-off Rule:**
   - Never say *"Now over to Speaker 2"*. 
   - Instead, hand off through an **action cue**: Speaker 1 sets up the exact screen state $\rightarrow$ Speaker 2 immediately picks up the breath and taps the screen.
3. **Pacing Rule:**
   - Speak 10% slower than you think you need to. Enunciate. Let the dramatic beats land.

## 💡 Keynote Presentation Secrets

1. **Volume on the Voice Note:**  
   When Speaker 3 triggers Shankar Lal’s Hindi voice note, make sure the laptop volume is at **100%**. Hearing that genuine Hindi voice in the auditorium creates an instant, unforgettable emotional connection.
2. **Smooth Cursor Movement:**  
   Speaker 2 should move the mouse deliberately. Avoid jittery scrolling. Let the judges absorb each card for 2 full seconds before clicking.
3. **The Eye-Contact Triangle:**  
   - Speaker 1 focuses primarily on **Bhavishya Pandit** (growth/impact) and **Aayush Arora** (vision/scale).
   - Speaker 3 locks eyes with **Vishal Soni** during the "deterministic engine vs. cognitive layer" architectural sentence. That single sentence will win the technical vote.
4. **No Apologies:**  
   If a network hiccup occurs, never say *"sorry, it’s not loading"*. Speaker 2 simply switches to the pre-loaded tab with a calm smile. Confidence wins hackathons.
