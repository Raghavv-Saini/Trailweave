# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Discreet, cultural travelers, heritage explorers, and artisan curators seeking authentic encounters in Udaipur, Rajasthan.

## Product Purpose
Authentic Udaipur travel discovery and deterministic, constraint-aware in-trip companion connecting travelers directly with living master craftsmen while keeping field itineraries realistically paced.

## Positioning
Unlike generic AI itinerary generators that hallucinate impossible transit times and market-trap tourist shops, TrailWeave pairs community-verified field trails with an AI companion that proposes changes verified by a deterministic engine against true venue hours, opening constraints, and verified local artisan studios.

## Operating Context
Travelers walking Udaipur's narrow pedestrian alleys around Lake Pichola, Gangaur Ghat, and the Old City, viewing under bright sunlight on mobile, or planning/curating comprehensive multi-day itineraries on desktop.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Community-curated trails with relative stop timelines and budget estimates
- Cherry-picking and "Use as Base" itinerary basket
- Deterministic constraint checking (opening hours, transit times, visit duration)
- AI active companion proposing non-destructive replans (user confirms)
- Artisan voice onboarding in Hindi with admin review and catalogue management
- Strict domain language adhering to GLOSSARY.md

## Brand Commitments
- Name: TrailWeave
- Aesthetic: Understated luxury editorial, Udaipur limestone ground, Pichola slate ink, Mewar indigo accents, Newsreader typography
- Zero AI hype slop: No gratuitous purple gradients, glowing sparkle badges, or robot emojis.

## Evidence on Hand
- Verified Udaipur places and artisan catalogue in `backend/app/db/seeds/` and `frontend/lib/mockData.ts`
- Deterministic engine in `backend/app/engine/`

## Product Principles
1. Deterministic Truth: The AI proposes; the deterministic engine verifies and commits.
2. Understated Luxury: Quiet, respectful editorial presentation rooted in Udaipur's living culture.
3. Field Durability: Clear readability under Rajasthan sunlight; fast, responsive, and mobile-friendly.
